Happy Birthday, Shimiya..

I don't even know what I'm supposed to write to you. There are so many things I've never said, so many things I've felt but never really found the words for. I've always told you that you're special to me but I've never told you why? What makes someone so special to another person? Is it their efforts, the way they behave, the way they listen or perhaps the little things they do without even realising how much they mean. Maybe it's all of that or maybe it's something that words will never quite be able to explain. There are so many moments with you that still lives somewhere in the back of my mind, and despite having so much to say here I am struggling to write even a single word. It's October 15th and perhaps I should've started writing this earlier but after overthinking every little thing a hundred times I'm finally here. Idk if I'll get everything right, if I'll say something unusual or if these words will even do justice to what I feel, but I suppose some things deserves to be written even when we don't know where to begin.

Let's start with how we met shall we? Two selenophiles, brought together by something as ordinary as a conversation. I remember thinking of texting you, though at first you were actually someone else I had intended to talk to. We ended our conversation and then out of nowhere you sent me a random reel. To this day I don't know whether it was a mistake or something you sent without a second thought but I'm glad you did. Cause somehow that little insignificant moment became the beginning of something I would come to cherish so deeply. The more I got to know you the more I discovered how alike we were. We shared the same music taste, the same interests and even the same dreams of becoming something someday. Sometimes I'd find myself thinking about something only to realise that you'd thought about it too, perhaps a little later but still. It felt as though I had found someone who understood parts of me that I never really knew how to explain. And somewhere along the way I found my "anam cara" someone who made me feel like perhaps I didn't have to spend another four years trying to find a connection like this. And the strange thing is even after all this time that feeling hasn't changed.

I've never forgotten the efforts you've made, the way you've treated me or the little things you've done that you probably don't even remember anymore. Perhaps you never realised how much they meant to me but I did. I remember them and I think a part of me always will. Maybe that's what makes someone special, not one grand gesture or a thousand beautiful words but the way they make you feel through the smallest things. The way they listen without making you feel foolish for speaking, the way they let you explain yourself without passing judgement and the way they make you feel heard when the rest of the world seems too busy to listen. Maybe it's knowing that even after disagreements, misunderstandings and arguments there's still a part of you that holds on to the bond you've built. I don't think I've ever been particularly good at explaining myself and perhaps I'll never find the courage to tell someone everything that goes on inside my head. But with you I found someone I could try with. Someone whose presence made the world feel a little less unfamiliar. And maybe without even realising it you became one of those rare people whose existence changes the way you look at friendship itself.

And perhaps that's why even after fivee years or more I still feel the same. I don't know what the future has written for us or how many more arguments, misunderstandings, distances or changes life has waiting around the corner. But I know that the time we've spent together means something to me and I know there are still so many moments we haven't lived yet. If I were asked to close my eyes and think about the people who have left a mark on my life, I have no doubt you'd find a place among those final seven minutes of memories. Maybe we haven't spent as much time together as we would've liked, maybe there are things we've never said and moments we wish had lasted a little longer but we have our little collection of memories and I'm grateful for every one of them. So on your eighteenth birthday I hope life gives you a thousand reasons to smile, takes you closer to every dream you've ever held and brings you the kind of happiness that stays long after the moment has passed. And if there's one thing I want you to know it's that among all the people I've met and all the connections I've made, you'll always be my anam cara, the friend who made an ordinary conversation feel like the beginning of something I never knew I was looking for. Happy birthday you little human. I'm glad that random reel found its way to me. ♡
